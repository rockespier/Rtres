# Product

## Register

brand

Primary surface is the public marketing site (`mockups/index.html`) — design IS the product there, since its whole job is to win new clients. The client portal (`mockups/portal/*`) is a secondary, product-register surface nested under the same visual system: forms and dashboards where design serves the task (manage products, file tickets) rather than sells. When working on portal screens specifically, apply `product` register conventions (restraint, clarity, task focus) layered on top of the brand system defined here — don't import brand-register flourishes (marketing copy tone, decorative motion) into portal task flows.

## Users

- **Prospects** (public site): small/medium business owners and marketing leads in Peru, Italy and elsewhere, evaluating who builds their web presence. They're comparing several agencies, skimming fast, and deciding based on how credible and current the site feels — the site's own design is the first proof point of Rtres's competence.
- **Existing clients** (portal): non-technical business owners who already contracted Rtres. Their context is transactional and occasional (checking a renewal date, filing a support ticket, paying a subscription) — not daily software use. They need to get in, do the task, and get out with confidence it worked.

## Product Purpose

A trilingual (ES/EN/IT) corporate site that converts visitors into leads for Rtres Web Solutions (18+ years, 300+ projects delivered: web design, e-commerce, SEO/SEM, custom development), paired with a client portal where existing clients manage contracted products (hosting, domain, SSL, backup, monthly support), pay PayPal subscriptions, and file support/change tickets that sync automatically to GitHub Issues. Success = more qualified leads from the public site, and existing clients self-serving product/ticket management instead of emailing Rtres directly.

## Brand Personality

Moderno, confiable, técnico (Modern, trustworthy, technical) — three words the user chose directly. Voice: expert but approachable, not playful or jokey; confidence earned from 18 years/300+ projects, not asserted through hype language. The site should read as current (2026 visual trends) without reading as a templated AI-generated agency site.

## Anti-references

- Generic "AI SaaS" template look: cream/beige neutral backgrounds, gradient text, uniform icon+heading+text card grids, a tiny uppercase eyebrow tag repeated above every single section.
- Anything that undercuts the "18 years of experience" credibility claim by looking like a 2-week no-code template.

## Design Principles

1. **Credibility through craft, not claims.** The site's own polish is the proof of "professionals with 18 years of experience" — sloppy details (misaligned grids, low-contrast text, generic patterns) directly contradict the sales pitch.
2. **One brand system, two registers.** Public site sells (brand register: bold type, motion, narrative); portal serves a task (product register: clarity, predictability, low cognitive load) — same tokens, different discipline.
3. **Trilingual parity.** ES/EN/IT are equal citizens, not a translated afterthought — layout must hold up when a language runs longer (Italian/Spanish text is typically longer than English).
4. **Real content over placeholder polish.** Pricing, service descriptions and FAQ answers are sourced from Rtres's actual materials (Documentacion/), not invented — keeps the "vanguardista" visual layer honest.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum: body text ≥4.5:1 contrast, large/display text ≥3:1, visible keyboard focus states, `prefers-reduced-motion` alternative for every animation. No specific assistive-tech user requirements beyond AA stated by the client.
